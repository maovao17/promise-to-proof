# First launch experiment
Audience is solo service founders who track inquiries in a spreadsheet or messages.
Position Customer Desk as a simple place to find a lead and see its follow-up status.
Draft invitation: “I’m testing a small customer desk for service businesses. Would you try adding, finding and updating a sample lead in a five-minute walkthrough?”
Ask three volunteers through existing relationships after the founder approves outreach. Measure task completion, time to find a record, duplicate errors, and whether they would use it again. Treat interest as a signal for a follow-up test.
