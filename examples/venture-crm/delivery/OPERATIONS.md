# Daily follow-up
1. Add an inbound customer and check that their email is unique.
2. Search for the customer before a follow-up.
3. Update status after each interaction.
4. At the end of the day, inspect New and Contacted entries and identify the next action.
5. Send unresolved duplicate or data-loss issues to the founder with reproduction steps.
The founder owns the follow-up queue. The builder owns fixes. The reviewer checks the fix against the same criteria.
