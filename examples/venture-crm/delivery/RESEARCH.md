# Alternatives and assumptions
[HubSpot record creation](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/create-records-on-the-updated-index-page) documents adding contacts in table and board views and using email to deduplicate contacts. A spreadsheet is another baseline, with columns for name, email and status.
Our hypothesis is that a solo founder values a focused follow-up view. This desk review establishes an existing workflow pattern. Demand and usability remain hypotheses.
Ask three service founders to show their current contact process. Have them add a fictional lead, find it, and update status. Record time, errors, and where their existing process works better.
